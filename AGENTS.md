# Working on PWFA

Read the [vision statement](docs/vision/vision-statement.md) first. It states what PWFA is for, what it is not, and how its validity is judged.

Follow the [project contribution guide](CONTRIBUTING.md). For research and architecture choices, check material mathematical assumptions with quick Lean experiments before presenting options. Include links to the reproducible checks and identify empirical questions that still need measurement. [Research workflow](CONTRIBUTING.md#lean-experiments-for-research-decisions) · [Owner decision](docs/decisions/ADR-117.md)

Put every choice that belongs to the owner as a multiple-choice question in plain, non-technical language. Give each option its pros and cons, rank the options by alignment with the original vision, feasibility and accuracy, and mark the recommended one. [How to ask](CONTRIBUTING.md#asking-the-owner-for-a-decision) · [Owner decision](docs/decisions/ADR-130.md)
