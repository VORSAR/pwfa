# Hypotheses and claims

| Statement | Status | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| Persistent wave dynamics are a genuinely useful computational primitive: information exists as evolving spatial and temporal patterns, and learning emerges through changes in the relationships between them | Central hypothesis of the [vision statement](vision-statement.md), not demonstrated | Mathematical consistency, reproducible experiments, and a measurable advantage against an established computational baseline |
| Nonlocal phase fields can implement the recovered storage/retrieval protocol | Research hypothesis, not demonstrated | Complete equations, implementation, controlled tests |
| Proposed global learned gain and homeostasis behave within specified bounds | Mathematical/numerical obligation, not proved | Domains, existence/stability analysis, convergence tests |
| PWFA exceeds a matched comparator in exact recall by at least five percentage points | Proposed preregistered success criterion (not an outcome) | Benchmark protocol, baseline, clustered interval lower bound >0 |
| Lean formal verification implies biological or cognitive validity | **Explicitly not claimed** | Would require independent empirical study |
| PWFA reproduces the brain's anatomy, imitates consciousness, or is biologically equivalent | **Explicitly not claimed**; excluded by the vision statement | None sought |

The design must distinguish a **hypothesis**, a **mathematical contract**, a **proof**, a **numerical observation**, and an **empirical result**.
