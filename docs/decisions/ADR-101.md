## Context

The recovered PWFA architecture calls for a continuous, circle-valued spatial phase field, nonlocal coupling, learned pairwise memory, homeostasis, and an encoding/decoding pathway. The original full evolution, memory, and decoding equations have **NOT BEEN RECOVERED**. We need a defensible way to develop a reviewable specification without claiming to know missing historical definitions.

## Alternatives presented at decision time

- **A — Constrained reconstruction (selected):** Retrieve original primary equations where possible; otherwise formulate new candidate continuous nonlocal phase-field and learned-memory equations constrained by recovered decisions. Explicitly label all new assumptions; seek new owner decisions and independent review.
- **B — Minimal mathematically closed reference first:** Specify a smaller periodic oscillator-memory system and extend it later toward spatial nonlocal PWFA. Easier initial proof scope, but may omit characteristic behavior.
- **C — Historical recovery first:** Defer new specifications until the original full equations and alternatives have been recovered. Maximizes fidelity only if original sources can be found; unknown recovery feasibility blocks progress.
- **Other / D — Discrete-only surrogate as canonical:** Use a finite-dimensional discrete system instead of the continuous model. Easier computational reasoning but changes the central research question.

## User decision

**Option A.** The owner explicitly selected constrained reconstruction, and instructed that this decision be recorded through GitHub ADR change control before proceeding to ranked subsequent decisions.

This accepts the *method for developing a candidate specification*. It does **not** approve any particular new mathematical equation, claim of scientific success, or implementation start.

## Contemporaneous assistant recommendation

**Option A**, recommended before the owner's selection on 2026-11-22.

Reasoning: the original project direction emphasizes continuous spatial phase dynamics, nonlocal coupling and learned memory; the missing transcripts cannot be assumed recoverable. Constrained reconstruction preserves these observable requirements while allowing progress. New assumptions remain distinguishable from historical recoveries.

## Rationale, tradeoffs, and consequences

- **Alignment:** preserves the recovered continuous phase, spatial coupling, memory, and homeostatic objectives as design constraints, without claiming exact historical equivalence.
- **Feasibility:** requires multiple mathematical choices, explicit domains, well-posedness investigation and independent review. A plausible equation is not automatically a mathematically viable or empirically effective model.
- **Versus B:** retains characteristic research scope at the cost of more proof obligations and complexity.
- **Versus C:** avoids indefinitely blocking on inaccessible source material; increases the burden of precise provenance.
- **Versus D:** retains the continuous-system hypothesis rather than replacing it with its discretization.
- No quantitative empirical comparative score was established; any prior 1–5 ranking was a qualitative judgment.

## Proof and test obligations

1. Make a normative state-space specification with spatial topology, circle-valued phase equivalence, regularity and initial conditions.
2. Propose an explicit nonlocal phase evolution law, operator/kernel, symbol domains, units and assumptions.
3. Specify pairwise memory evolution and homeostat dynamics consistent with [ADR-093](ADR-093.md) and [ADR-094](ADR-094.md), including denominator safety.
4. Complete event semantics, encoder/decoder, observation and solver specifications consistent with recovered ADRs.
5. Track each candidate choice through a new or amended ADR, associated requirements, mathematical contracts, counterexamples and proof/test obligations.
6. Independently examine well-posedness, stability where asserted, and observable numerical convergence; leave unproved claims unproved.
7. Obey [Gate 1](../verification/review-gates.md): no executable Lean 4 work until every independent Gate-1 finding, including documentation findings, is closed.
