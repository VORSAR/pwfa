# ADR-117 — Lean experiments in the research and decision process

Accepted on 2026-11-24. The owner requested a permanent practice of writing quick Lean checks for approaches, theories, and assumptions before presenting options. This applies across PWFA research. [Project workflow](../../CONTRIBUTING.md#lean-experiments-for-research-decisions) · [Experimental review policy](ADR-109.md)

## Decision

For a proposed approach with a material, formalizable assumption, check it in the pinned Lean environment before presenting the recommendation. Use the smallest statement that can distinguish the options. An exact example that exposes a failure can be as informative as a general theorem. Report the hypotheses, result, and implications alongside accuracy, feasibility, and alignment with the project objective. Where the outcome depends on data or dynamics beyond the checked model, identify the measurement that would settle it.

Keep decision-relevant checks in the repository and link them to the research note or ADR. CI runs the collection. A failed candidate can be kept as a compiling counterexample to the original conjecture. A proof that is still open belongs in the research record with its status. ADR-109 governs internal experimental authorization; the owner chooses the architecture and the independent reviewer assesses the frozen scope.

## First application

The bit-phase experiment checks the ideal readout, both signs under bounded relative phase error, the exact boundary tie, pooling cancellation, and an example distinguishing two ways to prepare a masked cue. These claims inform the phase-map and mask choices. Recall accuracy at the historical readout time needs a trained model and held-out cues.
