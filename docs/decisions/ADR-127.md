# ADR-127 — Paired training durations for the development pilot

Accepted on 2026-11-25. The owner selected A after reviewing the Lean duration checks. [Full-episode window](ADR-126.md) · [Duration evidence](../research/recall-pilot-checks.md#checked-duration-candidates)

## Decision

Train one condition for 25 model time units per word and the other for 50. Train each condition from its own fresh library state on the same four stored words in the same order. Use the same held-out development cues, corruption, decoder, and other settings for both. Compare exact-word recall at the same retrieval time $t=40$, with every scheduled cue counted in both conditions. Use the paired development results to select a duration before running the final benchmark on untouched data. Cue timing within an episode and the retrieval clock origin still need specification. [Scoring policy](ADR-122.md) · [Development benchmark](../experiments/benchmarks.md#first-trained-recall-measurement)

## Alternatives

- **A. Compare 25 and 50 on paired development cues.** The owner accepted the assistant’s recommendation. This measures whether longer training changes exact recall. With the same time step the two conditions together require three times the nominal training steps of 25 alone.
- **B. Start at 25 only.** This requires the least training time and leaves the longer exposure untested.
- **C. Start at 50 only.** This allows more homeostatic adaptation under a sustained drive and leaves the shorter exposure untested.

Lean checked that constant unit drive from a zero initial homeostat gives $1-e^{-1/2}$ and $1-e^{-1}$ after 25 and 50 units. These are illustrative conditional responses. The duration comparison has no trained outcome yet.
