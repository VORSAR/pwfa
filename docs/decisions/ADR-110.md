# ADR-110: Phase-lag law for the flat torus baseline

- **Status:** Accepted for the written baseline. Mathematical review is pending.
- **Decision date:** 2026-11-23
- **Selected option:** 1A
- **Provenance:** The owner selected 1A in the PWFA design discussion after reviewing the alternatives below.
- **Affects:** [Flat torus oscillator baseline](../verification/slices/torus-oscillator-baseline.md)
- **Related:** [ADR-076a](ADR-076a.md), [ADR-102](ADR-102.md), [ADR-103](ADR-103.md), [ADR-111](ADR-111.md)

## Context

The historical ring reference fixes a sine interaction with a phase lag. The first 2D baseline needs a concrete evolution equation on the approved flat torus so its mathematical properties can be checked before adding inputs or learning. [Historical ring reference](../mathematics/ring-oscillator-reference.md)

## Alternatives presented at decision time

- **1A — Transfer the recovered phase-lag law.** High alignment and high feasibility. Use uniform area measure and continuous unit phases. Gives exact rotation checks and commits the unforced baseline to a concrete law.
- **1B — Specify a family of phase interactions first.** Medium alignment and medium feasibility. Allows comparison of other interactions at the cost of additional work before a concrete baseline is available.

## Owner decision

Transfer the historical sine phase-lag law to the flat torus with uniform area measure of total mass one and continuous initial fields valued in the unit circle. Use the integral law in the [baseline](../verification/slices/torus-oscillator-baseline.md), with coupling strength entering once as the multiplier of the normalized interaction. The first slice is unforced and phase-only.

## Recommendation and consequences

The assistant recommended 1A because the recovered sign convention and exact rotating solutions make the transfer reviewable. This fixes the evolution law for the first slice. The operating values for simulations and any extension to inputs or learning are separate decisions.

## Proof and test obligations

Check local well-posedness and continuation, pointwise unit modulus, winding under continuous evolution, the rotation rate of a constant field, and the rates of the selected Fourier modes. Continue the internal review under [ADR-109](ADR-109.md). Independent Gate 1 is pending.
