# ADR-125 — Time average of signed alignment within a training episode

Accepted on 2026-11-25. The owner selected A for the first trained recall pilot after reviewing the Lean comparison of time averaging and a terminal exponential trace. [Signed pair alignment](ADR-123.md) · [Trace comparison](../research/recall-pilot-checks.md#what-the-trace-retains-within-one-episode)

## Decision

For every oscillator pair $(i,j)$ and a training window $[a,b]$ of positive duration, take the episode summary to be

$$
S_{ij}=\frac{1}{b-a}\int_a^b \operatorname{Re}(\overline{z_i(t)}z_j(t))\,dt.
$$

Each unit of elapsed time receives equal weight. When combining subwindow means, weight each by its elapsed duration. At the end of a completed episode, add its summary once to the equal-weight mean over words specified in ADR-124. The start and end events of the training window, its physical duration, and numerical quadrature for varying alignment remain open. [Across-episode decision](ADR-124.md) · [Training transitions](../mathematics/memory.md#training-and-recall-transitions)

## Alternatives

- **A. Time mean of signed alignment.** The owner selected the assistant’s recommendation. It gives equal influence per unit of exposure and needs no trace time constant for this summary.
- **B. Final value of an exponential trace.** This emphasizes later observations and would require a trace time constant.
- **C. Compare both summaries in paired runs.** This would test their effect on recall accuracy with an additional training condition and a chosen trace time constant.

In the checked example, alignment is +1 and −1 for equally long intervals. The time mean is zero in either order. A zero-start exponential trace with half retention over each interval ends at −1/4 or +1/4. Held-out exact-word recall at $t=40$ remains unmeasured. [Lean experiment](../../scripts/experiments/exponential-stage.lean) · [Recall endpoint](../experiments/benchmarks.md#first-trained-recall-measurement)

The follow-up Lean check verifies the exact time integral, signed bounds for unit phases, and composition across unequal subwindows. [Proofs and window examples](../research/recall-pilot-checks.md#exact-time-averages-on-a-training-window)
