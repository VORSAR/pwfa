# ADR-121 — Sample placement for encoder validation

Accepted on 2026-11-24. The owner selected option 1A after comparing exact periodic-grid coverage for all sixteen bit centers. [Grid sizes](ADR-112.md) · [Encoder layout](ADR-115.md) · [Anchor](ADR-116.md)

## Decision

In the primary runs, sample the unit torus at $(i/N_x,j/N_y)$, with zero-based indices. For the placement sensitivity check, sample at $((i+1/2)/N_x,(j+1/2)/N_y)$ modulo one. Keep the physical bit centers and origin fixed across placements. Record the grid and offset with each result. The half-shift requires reconstruction for point observations at those physical locations. Choosing the production interpolation and spatial readout rules remains open.

## Alternatives and evidence

- **1A. Origin-aligned primary, shifted sensitivity.** The assistant recommended this option and the owner selected it. It gives direct readings at all centers and the origin in the primary runs, and tests registration sensitivity in the repeat.
- **1B. Origin-aligned only.** This keeps direct readings and costs less, but leaves placement sensitivity unmeasured.
- **1C. Half-cell shifted primary.** More coarse-grid samples see each candidate bump, at the cost of reconstructing the center and anchor readings.

Lean checked alignment separately for every bit center and all three grids. The [experiment](../../scripts/experiments/encoder-grid.lean) and [counts by radius](../research/bit-to-phase-map.md#checked-node-registration-and-support-counts) describe point coverage. The choice of stimulus radius is a separate decision.
