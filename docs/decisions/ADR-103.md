# ADR-103: Unit-complex phase-state representation

- **Status:** Accepted by owner; independent Gate 1 OPEN
- **Decision date:** 2026-11-22
- **Selected option:** A (Batch 2, Decision 3)
- **Provenance:** Explicit owner selection in PWFA conversation, 2026-11-22; NEW decision.
- **Related:** [ADR-101](ADR-101.md), [ADR-102](ADR-102.md), [ADR-095](ADR-095.md), [ADR-097](ADR-097.md)

## Context and original alternatives
- **A:** Unit-complex phase z(x,t)=exp(iθ(x,t)) with |z|=1. Selected; prior assistant recommendation.
- **B:** Angles modulo 2π. Same underlying S¹ phase, with coordinate-wrapping considerations.
- **C:** Globally unwrapped real angle. Easier for some calculations but a continuous global lift need not exist in nonzero winding sectors.
- **Other:** Not selected.

## Owner decision
Represent the phase by a circle-valued field z : T² × time → S¹, where S¹={w∈C:|w|=1}. Angle coordinates θ may be local or defined globally only when a lift exists. The selection does not determine the phase evolution law or function-space regularity.

## Contemporaneous recommendation and tradeoffs
Assistant recommended **A**, ranked B second, C third. Benefits: phase topology is explicit, artificial coordinate wrap discontinuities are avoided, and this representation aligns with the recovered multiplicative phase-midpoint direction. Costs: the evolution and numerical scheme must preserve unit magnitude; winding still needs independent diagnostics. Ranking was qualitative, not measured.

## Proof and reconciliation obligations
1. Define admissible regularity and initial values for z:T²→S¹.
2. Propose an evolution tangent to S¹; prove unit-norm preservation under stated assumptions.
3. Define and verify discrete norm-preserving updates and convergence diagnostics.
4. Address the two winding indices of T²→S¹ in applicable regularity classes.
5. Keep angle lifts local where global lifts are unavailable.
6. Preserve independent Gate 1 restrictions on executable Lean work.
