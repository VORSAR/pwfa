# ADR-123 — Signed phase alignment for the first pairwise memory design

Accepted on 2026-11-25. The owner selected A after the exact phase-pair comparison. This is a new pilot design choice made under the reconstruction strategy. [Memory contract](../mathematics/memory.md) · [Lean checks](../research/recall-pilot-checks.md#pairwise-input-to-the-learning-rule)

## Decision

Use $C_{ij}=\operatorname{Re}(\overline{z_i}z_j)$ as the first candidate input to the training trace for the full pairwise memory. It is a signed, symmetric input on every pair. The historical scalar $m$ in the global gain has its own contract. Include words and their complements in the recall checks with the cue and the spatial origin present.

## Alternatives

- **A. Signed alignment first.** The owner accepted the assistant's recommendation. Equal and opposite bits give +1 and −1 at the primary alphabet. This retains the bit relation and needs one candidate memory design.
- **B. Add squared alignment as a coequal control.** This would isolate the effect of removing sign at the cost of another training and retrieval condition.
- **C. Investigate a directional statistic first.** It could retain orientation information, with additional choices about asymmetric learning and coupling.

At both alphabets complementing every symbol leaves all the ideal signed pair readings unchanged. Retrieval with a partial cue and the signed origin-relative decoder remains an empirical test. The selected statistic leaves open the temporal trace, learning rate, episode update, retrieval transition and how learned coupling enters the phase dynamics.
